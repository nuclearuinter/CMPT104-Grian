Looking for some free horror for Halloween? There's plenty to be found online! I've got four of my favourite horror series here to share with you.

Keep in mind the initial ⚠️'s! They're there to warn you about any content that may be upsetting for some (more so than the usual horror). Newer entries in any of these series may feature content that isn't listed in the content warnings if this page hasn't been updated yet. Stay safe! :)

The Pumpkin Rabbit Series

⚠️: (Fictional) Children are harmed!
Starting as just a disconnected short story from The Walten Files series, The Mysterious House (2020) follows Tammy and Ducky, two trick-or-treaters looking to hit one last house before Halloween ends. Ducky's more reluctant, but Tammy insists--after all, it's just one house...

As stated in the description, this series began as a quick Halloween project for Martin, the creator. It feels like a cautionary tale or urban legend about Halloween as it was experienced by the victim, not how you'd tell it around a campfire.
The art isn't as refined as it could be; however, I didn't put this video here as the sole entry, think of it more as... context.
