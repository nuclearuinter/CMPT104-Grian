Looking for some free horror for Halloween? There's plenty to be found online! I've got four of my favourite horror series here to share with you.

Keep in mind the initial ⚠️'s! They're there to warn you about any content that may be upsetting for some (more so than the usual horror). Newer entries in any of these series may feature content that isn't listed in the content warnings if this page hasn't been updated yet. Stay safe! :)

The Pumpkin Rabbit Series

⚠️: (Fictional) Children are harmed!
There are two full-fledged videos currently featuring the Pumpkin Rabbit, most notably the sequel to The Mysterious House (2020), The Return of the Pumpkin Rabbit (2024). These are disconnected, "non-canon" (Martin, in the description of TMH) short stories from The Walten Files.

It isn't necessary to watch TMH to understand its sequel, TRPR, as it exposits enough information at the start that viewers who haven't seen TMH can understand the setting. However, watching both in chronological order does make you appreciate how much Martin's art has improved!
To me, TMH feels like a cautionary tale or urban legend about Halloween as experienced by the victim, not how you'd tell it around a campfire. There's still a level of detachment from the horror, however, as none of the violence truly happens on screen, only the aftermath. Both are clearly based on old 60s Halloween specials, given the aspect ratio, the intros (especially title cards!), and the grainy effect that makes it seem that it'd been ripped off a tape.

What I really want to talk about is The Return of the Pumpkin Rabbit (2024), its sequel.
